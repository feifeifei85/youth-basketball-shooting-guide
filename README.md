# Youth Shooting Training Manual

[中文](README.zh-CN.md)

I am a middle school student who loves basketball.

Shooting looks simple, but there is a lot to learn. My coach has taught me many useful ideas. During practice, I also keep thinking about why a shot feels right, why it misses, and how I can improve it.

I made this small manual to save what I have learned and share it with other young players. I am still practicing and trying different methods, so this is not a professional coaching book or a perfect answer. It is my own basketball project, built from coaching, practice, observation, and my thoughts.

I also use tools such as AI to help organize notes, compare ideas, and make the manual easier to understand. Basketball still has to be learned on the court, one shot at a time.

Open [`Youth Shooting Training Manual`](https://feifeifei85.github.io/youth-basketball-shooting-guide) to read the manual.
